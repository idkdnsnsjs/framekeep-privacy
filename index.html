資料收集與使用

Framekeep 不要求建立帳號。截圖、錄屏、滾動截圖、即時回放和 OCR 會在 Mac 上處理。翻譯使用 macOS 的翻譯功能，系統可能需要向 Apple 下載語言套件。Framekeep 不會將截取內容或辨識出的文字傳給開發者，也沒有廣告追蹤或第三方分析 SDK。

macOS 權限

擷取畫面需要 macOS 的螢幕錄製權限。若你已授予輔助使用權限，Framekeep 可用它辨識游標下的介面區域，或協助辨識選區中的文字。你可以在「系統設定 → 隱私權與安全性」撤銷權限。撤銷螢幕錄製權限後，擷取功能將無法使用。

本機儲存與刪除

即時回放預設關閉。開啟後，最近的錄影片段暫存在本機，並依保留設定清理。你可以從選單列關閉回放緩衝，正常結束 Framekeep 時會清理回放快取。

偏好設定與儲存位置保存在本機。你可以在 Finder 刪除已儲存的截圖、錄影與文件。可編輯的 .snaprecord 文件保留未打碼原圖，匯出的 PNG 則會套用你已設定的打碼。

試用與買斷

免費試用、買斷與恢復購買由 Apple App Store 處理。Framekeep 使用 StoreKit 的交易資訊判斷試用期限與解鎖狀態。開發者無法取得你的付款卡資料。Apple App Store 隱私說明

網站與聯絡方式

本頁由 GitHub Pages 託管，託管服務可能依其隱私權聲明處理網站請求資料。如有隱私問題，可透過 Framekeep 隱私問題回報聯絡開發者。回報是公開的，請避免附上個人資料、交易資訊或私密截圖。

Framekeep Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · 繁體中文

Data use

Framekeep requires no account. Screenshots, recordings, scrolling captures, instant replay, and OCR are processed on your Mac. Translation uses macOS Translation, which may download language packs from Apple. Framekeep does not send captures or recognized text to the developer and does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.

macOS permissions

Capturing requires macOS screen recording permission. If you grant Accessibility access, Framekeep can use it to identify interface regions under the pointer or assist with text recognition in the selection. You can revoke permissions in System Settings under Privacy & Security. Capturing will stop working if you revoke screen recording permission.

Local storage and deletion

Instant replay is off by default. When enabled, recent recording segments are cached locally and cleared according to the retention settings. You can turn off buffering from the menu bar. Quitting Framekeep normally clears the replay cache.

Preferences and save locations are stored locally. You can delete saved captures, recordings, and documents in Finder. Editable .snaprecord documents retain the unredacted original. Exported PNGs apply the redactions you set.

Trial and purchases

Apple handles the trial, one-time purchase, and purchase restoration through the App Store. Framekeep uses StoreKit transaction information to determine trial expiry and unlock status. The developer does not receive your payment card information. Apple App Store privacy information

Website and contact

GitHub Pages hosts this page and may process website request data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Framekeep issue tracker. Issues are public. Please omit personal information, transaction details, and private screenshots.
